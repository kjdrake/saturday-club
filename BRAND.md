# Saturday Club · a 4th & Soul app

Saturday Club is the college-football pick’em app in the 4th & Soul family. Its identity shares the parent brand's split circular frame, cream-and-antique-gold palette, athletic lettering, and relaxed sports-lounge tone. The SC monogram and football laces distinguish this app within that family.

## Icon

Master artwork: [`dist/assets/brand/saturday-club-icon.png`](dist/assets/brand/saturday-club-icon.png), a 1254 × 1254 PNG with an opaque near-black background. The original generated master is preserved in the icon file; it is used consistently in navigation, browser tabs, Apple touch icons, and link previews. The simplified monogram avoids the fine record/microphone detail in the parent's full podcast illustration.

Use the complete badge with its existing safe margin. Keep the original aspect ratio, cream-left/gold-right ring, and letter colors. Display it in a square or softly rounded square. The current website uses 48px in the main navigation and 30px in its mobile header. Pair it with a separate Saturday Club wordmark where space allows; keep “by 4th & Soul” as the parent attribution.

## Shared palette

| Token | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--brand-charcoal` | `#151511` | Main background and browser chrome |
| `--brand-panel` | `#24221D` | Cards and panels |
| `--brand-gold` | `#C79B46` | Primary actions, ring, key accents |
| `--brand-ivory` | `#F1E8D5` | Primary text and light mark |
| `--brand-border` | `#514735` | Dividers and surface outlines |

These tokens live in `dist/style.css` and can be reused when the larger 4th & Soul website is built. Keep gold selective so scores, picks, and primary actions remain easy to distinguish. Team logos retain their official colors on cream badges.

## Tone and future structure

Warm, social, confident, and easygoing: “Pull up a chair. Pick a side.” Use athletic headings with readable body text. For the future parent website, show Saturday Club as a distinct app tile using this badge and wordmark; reserve the full “4th & Soul Podcast” logo for podcast content. This release adds the family identity but does not build or move the app into a new parent-site navigation structure.
